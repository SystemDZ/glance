Example 1 of 4 · a task with steps
How do I connect my domain to my new site?
Typical AI answer

Great question! Connecting a custom domain can seem daunting at first, but with the right approach it's very manageable. First, it's important to understand that there are several ways to do this, depending on your registrar and your hosting provider. Generally speaking, you'll want to log in to your domain registrar, locate the DNS settings (which may be called something different depending on the provider), and then add the appropriate records.

For the root domain you will typically add an A record pointing to your host's IP address, whereas for the www version you'll usually want a CNAME record. Keep in mind that DNS changes can take anywhere from a few minutes to 48 hours to propagate, so don't panic if it doesn't work right away. You should also make sure not to delete any MX records if you use email on that domain, since that could break your email. Additionally, you may want to set up a redirect from www to the root domain. If you run into any issues, please don't hesitate to let me know and I'd be happy to help troubleshoot!

  • The one dangerous thing (MX records) is buried in the middle
  • No actual values to enter
  • All steps at once, with no way to tell where you are

Example 2 of 4 · a decision
Which database should I use for my app?
Typical AI answer

The right database really depends on a number of factors, including your data model, your scale requirements, your team's familiarity with the technology, and your budget. Relational databases such as PostgreSQL and MySQL are well suited to structured data and complex queries, while document databases like MongoDB offer flexibility for evolving schemas. SQLite can be an excellent choice for small applications or prototypes, though it may not be suitable for high-concurrency workloads.

It's also worth considering managed services, which can reduce operational overhead, as well as the ecosystem of tools and libraries available. Ultimately, the best choice depends on your specific needs, and I would recommend evaluating several options before making a decision.

Example 3 of 4 · an error
My build fails with "Module not found: ./utils/format". Why?
Typical AI answer

This error can have several possible causes. It could be that the file doesn't exist at the specified path, or that there is a typo in the import statement. It's also possible that the file extension is missing or that your module resolution settings in tsconfig.json or webpack aren't configured correctly. Another potential cause is a case-sensitivity mismatch, which commonly occurs when moving between operating systems. You may also want to check that the dependency was installed correctly, that your node_modules folder isn't corrupted, and that your build cache isn't stale.

I'd suggest going through each of these possibilities one by one. If the issue persists, feel free to share more details and I can take a closer look!

  • Six possible causes, no ranking
  • Doesn't say which one is likely
  • No command to run
With glance

🔴 Likely cause: capital letter mismatch

Your file is Format.ts but the import says format. Linux builds are case-sensitive, your Mac is not.

Fix

Match the import to the file name

import { fmt } from "./utils/Format";
If that is not it

Check the path, the tsconfig paths setting, and clear the build cache.

  • The most likely cause, named
  • The fix is one copyable line
  • Other causes are collapsed, not deleted
Example 4 of 4 · a plan
Help me plan the launch of my product.
Typical AI answer

Launching a product successfully requires careful planning across a number of areas. You'll want to begin by clearly defining your target audience and your value proposition, and then build a landing page that communicates this effectively. In the weeks leading up to launch, it's a good idea to build an email list, reach out to potential early adopters, and prepare your marketing materials, including social media content and a press kit. On launch day itself, you should coordinate announcements across your channels and be ready to respond to feedback quickly.

After launch, be sure to gather feedback, track your key metrics, and iterate on your product accordingly. Every product is different, so you may need to adapt this approach to your circumstances.
